\section{Evaluation Plan}

%% A2Z Q-S: Quality, Related Methods, Simulation Setup
In accordance with proposal-stage academic integrity, no experimental results are fabricated; this section specifies our planned evaluation protocol across two complementary benchmarks:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Microsoft GUIDE}~\cite{freitas2024}: Approximately 1M labeled incidents (707k training incidents: 20.6\% TP, 49.0\% BP, 30.4\% FP; 432 ATT\&CK techniques). To prevent data leakage~\cite{arp2022,engelen2021}, splits are strictly grouped by incident ID, organization, and time.
    \item \textbf{ExCyTIn-Bench}~\cite{wu2026}: 57 SQL telemetry tables across 8 real attack chains for evaluating multi-step SQL investigation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hyperparameter Tuning and Expected Outcomes}
All thresholds ($\tau$, $\tau_{\text{safety}}$), fusion weights ($w_{\text{ml}}$, $w_{\text{llm}}$), and tool-call budget $B$ will be tuned exclusively on the validation split. As outlined in Table~\ref{tab:eval_plan}, we expect the hybrid agent to: (1) achieve a lower TP miss rate than standalone GBDT and unaugmented LLM baselines; (2) trace a tunable Pareto frontier between miss rate and human escalation rate; and (3) improve ATT\&CK mapping accuracy and report auditability.
