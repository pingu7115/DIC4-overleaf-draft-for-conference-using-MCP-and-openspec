\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

%% A2Z W-Z: What proposed, eXcel, Yields, Zen
This paper presented the design of the AI SOC Analyst Agent, an evidence-grounded and escalation-aware hybrid framework that unifies calibrated GBDT classification, MITRE ATT\&CK domain knowledge, and bounded ReAct LLM reasoning. By operationalizing the Assume Breach paradigm and asymmetric error costs through deterministic high-risk rules (R1--R5) and safety relabeling ($\tau_{\text{safety}}$), the proposed architecture addresses both the lack of causal context in statistical ML and the high miss rates and premature closure of autonomous LLM agents.

Future work will execute the planned E1--E5 evaluation on Microsoft GUIDE and ExCyTIn-Bench, address GUIDE's heavily anonymized entity fields through DuckDB aggregation, extend the knowledge base to MITRE ATT\&CK for ICS to support AIoT edge environments, and validate deployment in live SIEM pipelines.
